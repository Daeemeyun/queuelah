## Description (max 4000 chars)

Tired of walking to your favourite hawker centre only to find a massive queue? QueueLah lets you check live queue lengths before you leave — reported by real people, right now.

**See what's happening before you go**
Browse live queue reports for hawker centres, food courts, and restaurants across Singapore. Reports are crowd-sourced and refreshed every 30 minutes so you always know what to expect.

**Report queues. Earn rewards.**
Spotted a short queue at Maxwell? A long one at Lau Pa Sat? Submit a quick report and earn points. Build a daily reporting streak and unlock exclusive badges. The more you contribute, the higher you climb on the city-wide leaderboard.

**Discover eateries on the map**
Tap any marker on the map to see live queue status, stall listings, community reviews, and historical busy hours — so you can plan your makan around the crowd.

**Badges and achievements**
- 🏆 Kiasu Kaki — first report of the day
- 🦸 Hawker Hero — 100 hawker centre reports
- 🗺️ Makan Explorer — visit 20 different eateries
- 🔥 Week Streak & Month Streak — report every day
- 👑 Queue King — earn 1,000 points

**Community forum**
Share feedback, report bugs, or suggest new features directly in the app. The QueueLah team reads every post.

**Save your favourites**
Star the eateries you visit most. Your saved list shows live queue status at a glance so you can pick the best time to head down.

**Your own avatar**
Customise your profile with hats, eyewear, companions, and floating items. Equip your Kopitiam Cap, slip on your Aviators, and bring your Baby Blob companion along for the ride.

QueueLah is free to use. No account required to browse — sign up to report queues, earn points, and join the community.

## Screenshot Requirements

Apple requires screenshots for the following sizes. You only need to shoot on one device — the largest — and App Store Connect will scale for the rest.

### Required
| Device | Resolution | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 16 Pro Max (6.9") | 1320 × 2868 px | Covers all modern iPhones |
| iPad Pro 13" (M4) | 2064 × 2752 px | Required because supportsTablet = true in app.json |

### Recommended screens to capture (in this order)
1. **Home screen** — show featured eateries and live queue statuses
2. **Map screen** — show markers with queue-level colour coding
3. **Eatery detail** — show queue status, trend chart, stall list
4. **Report screen** — show the queue reporting flow
5. **Leaderboard / Profile** — show the avatar, points, badges, and rank

### Tips
- Use a real device or a simulator with Singapore data seeded
- Capture in light mode (the app's default)
- Add a simple headline overlay on each screenshot in Figma or Canva before uploading (e.g. "Know the queue before you go")
- App Store Connect accepts PNG or JPEG

## App Store Connect — Fields to fill in eas.json once your account is active

After creating the app record in App Store Connect you will see:

- **Apple ID (ASC App ID):** A 10-digit number on the app's page → put in `eas.json` under `submit.production.ios.ascAppId`
- **Apple Team ID:** developer.apple.com → Account → Membership → Team ID → put in `eas.json` under `submit.production.ios.appleTeamId`
