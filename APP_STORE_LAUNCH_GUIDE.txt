BEFORE YOU CAN LAUNCH — MUST-FIX LIST
──────────────────────────────────────
These will cause App Store rejection if not done:

1. PAYMENTS — The "Upgrade to Pro" button currently does nothing.
   You need RevenueCat (recommended) or Stripe to process subscriptions.
   RevenueCat handles all the App Store In-App Purchase rules automatically.
   Website: revenuecat.com — free tier covers early-stage apps.

2. PRIVACY POLICY — Apple requires a real, hosted privacy policy URL.
   Use iubenda.com or termly.io to generate one in ~10 minutes.
   Host it on your website or a free GitHub Pages page.

3. TERMS OF SERVICE — Same requirement. Generate alongside privacy policy.

4. AVATAR POSITIONING — Hat, eyewear, float items were positioned by estimate.
   Test on a real iPhone to confirm they look right before submitting.

5. PUSH NOTIFICATIONS — Partially set up. Need to wire up Expo push tokens
   so notifications actually send to users.

6. SEED THE DATABASE — App looks empty on first open. Add at least 20–30
   real eateries in Singapore so new users see content immediately.

PHASE 1 — FINISH THE APP
================================================================================

Complete all items in the Must-Fix List above.
Run the app on a physical iPhone (not simulator) and walk through every screen.
Fix any crashes, broken buttons, or ugly layouts you find.

Estimated time: 2–4 weeks depending on RevenueCat integration complexity.

PHASE 2 — APPLE DEVELOPER ACCOUNT
================================================================================

Cost:    USD $99/year (approximately SGD $135/year)
Website: developer.apple.com/enroll

Steps:
  1. Go to developer.apple.com/enroll
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID (or create one)
  3. Choose "Individual" (unless registering as a company)
  4. Pay the $99 fee
  5. Wait 24–48 hours for Apple to approve your account

You need this before you can do anything else.
If you register as a company, Apple will verify your business — takes longer.


================================================================================
PHASE 3 — BUILD THE APP FILE (.ipa)
================================================================================

What is this?
  An .ipa file is the compiled iPhone app — like a .exe for Windows but for iOS.
  You cannot build this on your Mac directly. You use a service called EAS Build
  (Expo Application Services) which compiles it in the cloud.

Steps (your developer runs these):
  1. Install EAS CLI:       npm install -g eas-cli
  2. Login to Expo account: eas login
  3. Configure the build:   eas build:configure
  4. Start the build:       eas build --platform ios
  5. EAS will ask for your Apple Developer credentials — enter them
  6. Wait ~20–30 minutes for the build to complete
  7. Download the .ipa file from the EAS dashboard

Cost: EAS Build free tier gives you 30 builds/month. More than enough.
Website: expo.dev


================================================================================
PHASE 4 — APP STORE CONNECT SETUP
================================================================================

App Store Connect is Apple's dashboard for managing your app listing.
Website: appstoreconnect.apple.com

Steps:
  1. Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com and sign in
  2. Click the "+" button to create a new app
  3. Fill in:
       - Platform: iOS
       - Name: QueueLah (this is what users see on the App Store)
       - Primary Language: English
       - Bundle ID: (your developer will know this — it's in app.json)
       - SKU: any unique code you make up, e.g. "queuelah-001"

  4. Fill in the App Information page:
       - Subtitle: "Skip the Queue in Singapore" (max 30 chars)
       - Category: Food & Drink (primary) or Navigation (secondary)
       - Privacy Policy URL: your hosted privacy policy link

  5. Fill in the Description (max 4000 characters):
       Write what the app does, who it's for, key features.
       First few lines are most important — users see these first.

  6. Keywords (max 100 characters total, comma-separated):
       "queue,hawker,singapore,food,wait time,kopitiam,eatery"
       Good keywords = more people find your app via search.

  7. Support URL: your website or a contact email page

  8. Copyright: "2026 [Your Name / Company Name]"

PHASE 5 — APP STORE ASSETS (SCREENSHOTS & ICON)
================================================================================

App Icon:
  - Size: 1024 × 1024 pixels, PNG, no transparency, no rounded corners
    (Apple rounds the corners automatically)
  - Use your QueueLah logo/icon at full resolution

Screenshots (mandatory):
  Apple requires screenshots for specific iPhone sizes.
  Required sizes:
    - iPhone 6.9" (iPhone 16 Pro Max): 1320 × 2868 pixels
    - iPhone 6.5" (iPhone 11 Pro Max):  1242 × 2688 pixels

  You need at least 1 screenshot per size, up to 10.
  Best practice: 4–5 screenshots showing your best features.

Tips for good screenshots:
  - Show real content (real eateries, real queue data)
  - Add short text captions explaining each screen
  - Use Figma or Canva to create polished mockup frames
  - First screenshot is most important — it shows in search results

Optional:
  - App Preview Video (30 seconds max) — not required but boosts installs
  - iPad screenshots — only needed if you support iPad


================================================================================
PHASE 6 — TESTFLIGHT BETA TESTING
================================================================================

What is TestFlight?
  Apple's official beta testing platform. Real users install your app via
  TestFlight before it goes live on the App Store. This is mandatory
  for finding bugs before public launch.

Steps:
  1. In App Store Connect, go to your app → TestFlight tab
  2. Upload your .ipa build (from Phase 3)
  3. Apple runs an automated review (~1–2 days) — less strict than full review
  4. Once approved, go to "External Testing" and add testers by email
  5. Testers install TestFlight app on their iPhone, then install your app

Who to invite:
  - Friends, family, loyal early users
  - Aim for 10–20 testers minimum
  - Ask them to use every feature and report anything broken

How long:
  Run beta for at least 1–2 weeks. Fix bugs. Build a new version. Repeat.
  You can upload new builds anytime — testers get notified automatically.


================================================================================
PHASE 7 — SUBMIT FOR APP STORE REVIEW
================================================================================

When your beta testing is done and bugs are fixed:

Steps:
  1. In App Store Connect, go to your app → "1.0 Prepare for Submission"
  2. Fill in any remaining fields (all marked with orange dots must be complete)
  3. Select your build (the .ipa you uploaded)
  4. Click "Submit for Review"

What Apple checks:
  - Does the app crash?
  - Does every button work?
  - Is the privacy policy real and accessible?
  - Are in-app purchases set up correctly? (No fake "coming soon" buttons)
  - Does the app do what the description says?
  - Is the content appropriate?

Review time: Usually 1–3 business days. Can be faster (24hr) or slower (1 week).

If rejected:
  Apple sends an email explaining exactly why. Fix the issue, resubmit.
  Common rejections: broken payments, missing privacy policy, crashes on review.
  This is normal — many apps get rejected once before getting approved.


================================================================================
PHASE 8 — LAUNCH
================================================================================

Once Apple approves your app, you have 3 choices:

  A) Release Immediately — App goes live as soon as approved (default)
  B) Scheduled Release — You pick a specific date/time for launch
  C) Manual Release — App sits approved but not live until you click "Release"

Recommendation: Choose Manual Release so you can control exactly when
you announce it and when it goes live. You want to announce on social media
and release at the same time for maximum impact.

Launch day checklist:
  □ Post on social media (Instagram, TikTok, Reddit r/singapore)
  □ Message any hawker owners or food bloggers you know
  □ Send to all your beta testers to leave reviews
  □ Monitor for crashes (use Sentry or Expo's built-in crash reporting)


================================================================================
PHASE 9 — AFTER LAUNCH
================================================================================

First week:
  - Watch crash logs daily (Sentry.io is free to start)
  - Respond to every App Store review, especially negative ones
  - Fix any critical bugs and submit an update immediately
  - App Store updates also go through review but usually approved in ~24hrs

Growing the app:
  - Ask users to rate the app (use expo-store-review to trigger the prompt)
  - More 5-star reviews = higher ranking in App Store search
  - Share on Reddit r/singapore, HardwareZone food forums, local food Telegram groups
  - Reach out to local food bloggers for coverage

Android (Google Play):
  - Similar process but cheaper: one-time $25 USD fee (not yearly)
  - Review process is faster (usually same day to 3 days)
  - Build command: eas build --platform android
  - You can run iOS and Android in parallel once you're ready
