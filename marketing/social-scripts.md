# TikTok / IG Reels Scripts — QueueLah

Four shootable scripts. All phone-shot, solo-founder realistic, one idea per video. Post natively to both TikTok and IG Reels (re-export without watermark). On-screen text written explicitly — most viewers watch muted.

**Hashtag set (verified active June 2026 — mix, don't use all per post; 5–8 max):**
Broad: `#sgfood #sgfoodie #singaporefood #sgeats #foodsg`
Niche/specific: `#hawkerfood #hawkercentre #maxwellfoodcentre #laupasat #cbdlunch #sgtiktok #madeinsingapore`

**Filming permission note:** hawker centres are public-ish but film people's faces sparingly; shoot queues from behind/at distance. Don't show identifiable strangers prominently.

---

## Video 1 — THE LIVE MAXWELL DEMO (film this first)

**Why first:** easiest to shoot (one lunch trip, no acting), and it IS the product pitch — "watch the map match reality" in 15 seconds. Also generates the proof screenshot needed for Telegram/press.

- **Platform:** TikTok + Reels
- **Audience/angle:** CBD lunch crowd; the real-time proof angle
- **Length:** 12–18s

**Hook (first 1.5s):** Hard cut to a long queue at Maxwell, phone pans down the line.
On-screen text: **"This queue is on a live map right now"**

**Beats:**
1. (0–3s) Pan along a real lunch queue (Tian Tian or any long one).
2. (3–7s) Cut to phone screen: open QueueLah, map of Maxwell, tap the stall — queue status showing. Screen-record this part for clarity, intercut with the real queue.
3. (7–12s) Side-by-side or quick cuts: real queue ↔ app status. On-screen: **"reported by people actually here"**
4. (12–16s) You, deadpan to camera at your table with food: "Check before you walk over. That's it. That's the app." On-screen: **"QueueLah · free · no ads · no sign-up to browse"**

**Caption:**
> Built a free app that shows hawker queue lengths live. No account needed to browse. iOS, link in bio. Strongest in the CBD for now (Maxwell / Lau Pa Sat / Amoy) — it's new, so come seed your hawker centre 🙏

**Hashtags:** `#sgfood #hawkerfood #maxwellfoodcentre #sgfoodie #cbdlunch #madeinsingapore`

## Video 2 — THE LUNCH GAMBLE SKIT

- **Platform:** TikTok + Reels
- **Audience/angle:** every office worker; the relatable-pain angle
- **Length:** 20–25s

**Hook (first 1.5s):** You walking out of an office building, determined.
On-screen text: **"POV: you have exactly 1 hour for lunch"**

**Beats:**
1. (0–3s) Confident walk out of office lobby. Text: **"12:01 — chicken rice. today is the day."**
2. (3–8s) Walking montage, sped up. Text: **"12:11 — almost there"**
3. (8–12s) Arrive. Slow pan up an enormous queue. Your face falls. Text: **"12:12 — no."**
4. (12–16s) Defeated walk back. Sad zoom on a sandwich/cup noodles at desk. Text: **"12:35 — lunch."**
5. (16–22s) Cut: same opening shot, but you check your phone first. App shows queue = long at stall A, short at stall B. You walk the other way, get food fast. Text: **"or just… check first"** → **"QueueLah · free · iOS"**

**Caption:**
> The CBD lunch gamble ends here. I got tired of losing, so I built the app myself. Free, no ads, browse without an account. Link in bio. (iOS for now, Android people I hear you)

**Hashtags:** `#sgtiktok #sgfood #cbdlunch #hawkerfood #sgfoodie #officelife`

## Video 3 — SOLO FOUNDER STORY

- **Platform:** Reels-leaning (also TikTok)
- **Audience/angle:** indie-supporting Singaporeans; trust/credibility angle
- **Length:** 30–40s, talking to camera at a hawker table

**Hook (first 1.5s):** You at a hawker centre, food in front of you, direct to camera:
**"I spent 6 months building an app that Singapore maybe doesn't need. Let me explain."**

**Beats:**
1. (0–5s) Hook above. Text mirrors the spoken line.
2. (5–15s) "Every week I'd walk to Maxwell on my lunch break, see the queue, walk back, eat something sad at my desk. After the hundredth time I thought — why can't I just see the queue before I leave?" (B-roll: queue shots, your desk, the app.)
3. (15–25s) "So I built QueueLah. Live map, people report queues, everyone benefits. It's free, no ads — I'm one person, not a startup with investors. You don't even need an account to look."
4. (25–35s) "It only works if people report, so it's strongest in the CBD right now. If you eat at Maxwell, Lau Pa Sat or Amoy — you're literally the launch team." Text: **"link in bio · iOS · free"**

**Caption:**
> Solo dev, one app, zero ads. QueueLah shows live hawker queue statuses — strongest in the CBD for now because that's where the first reporters are. Browse free, no account. iOS. If it's empty in your area, that's your cue 😄

**Hashtags:** `#madeinsingapore #sgfood #hawkerfood #indiedev #sgfoodie #buildinpublic`

## Video 4 — THE LEADERBOARD FLEX

- **Platform:** TikTok-leaning
- **Audience/angle:** gamification crowd / kiasu completionists; retention + reporter recruitment
- **Length:** 12–15s
- **⚠️ Truthfulness gate:** only film once the leaderboard has real users on it. Do not fake entries.

**Hook (first 1.5s):** Close-up of phone showing leaderboard, your name at #1.
On-screen text: **"I'm the #1 queue reporter at Maxwell and nobody can take this from me"**

**Beats:**

2. (3–8s) Quick cuts: you at the hawker centre tapping a queue report, points animation, badge unlock. Text: **"report queues → earn points → eternal glory"**
3. (8–13s) Deadpan to camera: "It's a free app about hawker queues and I will defend this ranking with my life." Text: **"QueueLah · come take my crown · link in bio"**

**Caption:**
> Made a hawker queue app, made a leaderboard, became #1 by default because I'm the dev. Come humble me. Free, iOS, no account needed to browse — account only if you want my crown.

## Production order & cadence

1. **Film Video 1 first** — one lunch trip, no acting, doubles as proof material for Telegram admins and press. Everything else can wait; this can't.
2. Video 2 next (needs a colleague or tripod for two shots, still one location day).
3. Video 3 when comfortable on camera; Video 4 once the leaderboard has real users.
4. Realistic cadence for one person: **2 videos/week max.** Reuse b-roll aggressively.
5. Bio link: App Store link the moment it's live. Until then, hold posts — don't post with no link.
