## Outreach rules

- One message, one polite follow-up after 4–5 days, then stop. No spamming.
- Verify each bio's stated business-contact method before sending; DM only where DMs are clearly open for business enquiries.
- Never imply payment that isn't available. If a meal is comped, say in the brief that disclosure is required.
- Stagger sends: top 3 first; wait for responses before working down the list (avoids two creators posting "first look" simultaneously).
- Send Tue–Thu, late morning SGT (creators check DMs before lunch content scouting).
