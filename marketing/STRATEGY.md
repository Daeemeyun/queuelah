# QueueLah Launch Strategy

*CMO master plan. Everything else in this folder executes this. Last updated: 2026-06-05.*

## The strategy in three sentences

Seed a dense core of real queue-reporters at **Maxwell, Lau Pa Sat and Amoy** (CBD lunch crowd) via one honest founder post on Reddit, so the map demonstrably works somewhere. Use that proof — "X reports in Y days, map alive at Maxwell" — to broaden into Facebook groups, Telegram features and lifestyle press in weeks 2–3. Week-1 success = 30–100 active reporters concentrated in the CBD, not downloads.

## Why this wedge

- Fixed lunch window = sharpest queue pain in Singapore.
- Geographically dense = solves the chicken-and-egg map problem.
- Office workers overlap heavily with r/askSingapore / r/singapore.
- "Works at Maxwell" is a demonstrable claim; "covers Singapore" is an empty one.

**Budget:** start at $0. Unlock spend only on signal — first candidate: a paid Kiasu Foodies Telegram feature (~week 3) if the free channels show the CBD wedge converting.

## Operating model (hands-off for Damien)

- **Automated:** a daily 8:30am scheduled task ("queuelah-marketing-ops") checks Gmail for Apple review news + mentions, reads this folder's calendar, and outputs the day's to-do with paste-ready content. See OPS-PLAYBOOK.md.
- **Damien's only job:** ~10 min/day — paste/send what the digest queues, reply to comments as the founder, report queues when he's at lunch. Founder voice can't be automated; everything else is.

## Hard rules

No sockpuppets, no fake reviews, founder posts as founder, disclose anything paid, never claim coverage the map doesn't have. SG communities screenshot inauthenticity — these rules ARE the strategy.

## What success looks like

- **Week 1:** few hundred downloads, 30–100 active reporters, map alive at 2–3 CBD centres at lunch. Quiet is normal.
- **Week 4:** a press hit or two, 1–2 creator videos, a number worth quoting, retention signal at the wedge locations.
