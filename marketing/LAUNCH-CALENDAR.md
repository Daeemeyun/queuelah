## Rules baked into this calendar

- Never broad-reach before local density.
- No day needs more than ~2 h; most need 10–15 min.
- Press waits for numbers (except Vulcan Post / Tech in Asia).
- Post days are kept light elsewhere so there's time to reply — replying IS the marketing.
