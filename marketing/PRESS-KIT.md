## Boilerplate (one paragraph — copy-paste ready)

> **QueueLah** is a free iOS app that shows live, crowd-reported queue lengths at Singapore's hawker centres, food courts and kopitiams on a map — so you can see the queue before you walk over. Anyone can browse with no account, no ads and no paywall; users who sign up can report queue lengths and earn points, badges and a leaderboard spot. Built solo by a Singaporean indie developer, QueueLah launched on the App Store in June 2026, starting with the CBD lunch crowd at Maxwell, Lau Pa Sat and Amoy Street.

*Short version (for tight word counts):*
> QueueLah is a free iOS app showing live, crowd-sourced hawker-queue lengths on a map across Singapore — see the queue before you walk over. Free, no ads, no sign-up to browse.

## The hook for journalists (why this is a story, not an ad)

- **Relatable, hyper-local pain:** queueing is a Singaporean national sport; everyone has a hawker-queue horror story. Easy, fun angle for lifestyle/tech desks.
- **Solo indie maker:** "Singaporean builds the fix himself, free, no investors" is a story local outlets root for.
- **The interesting bit:** the deliberate *launch-narrow-and-dense* strategy to solve the chicken-and-egg crowd-sourcing problem — a genuinely interesting product-thinking angle for tech press (Vulcan Post, Tech in Asia).
