## 3. Link strategy (the clickable bit)

Goal: one link that works for **both iPhone users (→ App Store) and everyone else (→ what-is-this page)**, because TikTok/IG traffic is mixed-device and an App Store link is a dead end on Android/desktop.

**Recommended: point the bio link at `https://queuelah.net`** (the landing page), and make sure that page has a prominent "Download on the App Store" button up top. This way:
- iPhone users land, tap the button, install.
- Android/desktop users land, understand the product, and can be captured (email/notify-me) for when Android ships.

**Why not link straight to the App Store?** It half-works — Android/desktop visitors hit a wall and bounce. queuelah.net as the hub is strictly better and costs nothing.

**If you'd rather use a link-in-bio tool** (only worth it once you have 2+ destinations): a free Linktree/Beacons/Bento page with two buttons — "Download (iOS)" → App Store, "What is QueueLah?" → queuelah.net. **For now, plain `queuelah.net` is simpler and faster — start there.**

**TikTok note:** the clickable website link in bio unlocks at the account level once you're on a Business account (no follower minimum currently). If for any reason yours is gated, put `queuelah.net` as plain text in the bio plus "link in bio" in captions until it unlocks.
